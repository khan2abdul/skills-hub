How “auto-approved” actually works

A skill has no approval flag of its own. dsh decides approval from the session permission preset, not from the skill: read-only asks before every write, workspace-write asks before writes outside the workspace, and danger-full-access never asks. This profile runs danger-full-access with approval: never, so no skill triggers a permission prompt here.

What a skill can do is carry its own written gate: a step that tells the agent to stop and get your consent before a destructive or irreversible act. Those are marked stop and ask below, and it is the only per-skill approval signal that exists. Everything else is the preset's call.

All 42 are auto-invocable: no skill sets disable-model-invocation, so the agent may load any of them without being told to. You can also call any one by name, e.g. /ship-it.

This is the whole non-bundled catalog. The retired 5-stack roots are unconfigured: D:/repo/setupskills/active_skills (the addyosmani, caveman, gstack and legacy repos) and ~/.agents/skills (ponytail) are gone from discovery, and includeDefaultRoots: false keeps them out. dsh's own 12 bundled skills — the office* set, the three cordis authoring skills, agent-experience, diagnose-windows-sandbox-acl — are still enabled and are not listed here, because they ship with the harness rather than this hub.

What this page does not claim

  • Guidance is derived, not quoted. The prose above condenses each skill's own text. The skill body is the authority; this page is an index. Skill bodies are immutable in this repo and are proven against upstream with tools/verify.py.
  • Only 13 of the 42 ship an explicit “When to Use” heading, and just one ships a “When not to Use” heading. The negative guidance here is assembled from each skill's stated exclusions, red flags and hard constraints. Where a skill states none, the entry is limited to what its text clearly supports rather than invented.
  • The approval model is the harness's, not the skill's. Changing the session preset changes every row of the approval picture at once. A skill's written gate is a instruction to the agent, not an enforced intercept.
  • Skill names are directory names under skills/. Invoke one with the skill tool or by typing /name.
